# Clinical Safety Note

NutriRisk AI is a research and portfolio prototype. It is not a medical device and must not be used for patient care.

Safe framing:

- The system prioritizes records for review.
- The system explains risk drivers.
- The system does not diagnose malnutrition.
- The system does not recommend treatment.
- A qualified clinician must review all evidence.

Recommended UI banner:

> Research demo only. Not for diagnosis, treatment, or clinical use.

Production requirements before real-world use would include clinical validation, governance approval, privacy/security review, bias evaluation, monitoring, incident response, and regulatory review.
